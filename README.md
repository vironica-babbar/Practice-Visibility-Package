# Practice Visibility Package (PVP)

Healthcare practices in the United States are run by passionate providers who
want to save lives and make a difference. This package helps them do just
that, by making sure patients can find them. It covers the channels that
matter most, step by step, in a few focused hours a week. It's especially
designed for practices that don't want to pay hundreds or thousands of dollars
to agencies or consultants.

Use the templates on their own, or paste any file into Claude along with your
fact sheet and ask Claude to fill in the blanks for you. You then paste the
results into each platform.

**Who is this for:** practice owners, office managers, front-desk executives,
patient experience executives and solo clinic owners at:

1. Primary care practices (PCPs)
2. Mental health clinics
3. Psychiatric practices
4. OB/GYN and dermatology clinics
5. Pediatric practices
6. Rehab facilities

Physician assistants (PAs) working in any of these practices are covered too.

> Primary care templates are being added to each module.

**What it covers:** 360-degree practice visibility. This package helps you get
listed correctly, accurately and in detail on multiple platforms. It does not
cover ad strategies, social media strategies, blog topics or analytics.

---

## How to use this package

1. **Start with `00-start-here/practice-fact-sheet.md`.** Fill it in once.
   It holds your official name, address, phone, hours, providers and
   insurance. Every other template uses the same blanks (for example
   `[PRACTICE NAME]`), so you copy your answers from this one sheet.
2. **Read `00-start-here/rules-for-every-module.md`** and skim
   **`copywriting-playbook.md`**. The rules page is one page, and it explains
   the rules that keep you out of trouble (privacy, claims, reviews).
3. **Open a module folder and work top to bottom.** Each template tells you:
   - **Where to paste it:** the exact screen or field
   - **The limit:** how many characters the field allows, and when that was
     last checked
   - **Your version:** pick the block that matches your practice type
4. **Replace every `[BLANK]`**, including the brackets. Before you paste,
   search the text for `[` to make sure you haven't missed one.

## Platform limits change

Google, Apple, directories and insurers change their character limits and
rules without notice. Every module shows a **"Last checked"** date and a
source link. If a date is more than about 6 months old, confirm the limit on
the platform itself before relying on it. The character counter inside each
platform's editor always wins over this package.

## Important: this package is not legal or compliance advice

These templates are written to support HIPAA, professional ethics codes and
platform rules, but they **do not replace review by your compliance officer,
attorney or licensing board.** Rules differ by state, license type and
situation (for example, substance use programs covered by 42 CFR Part 2 have
stricter confidentiality rules than HIPAA alone). Have someone qualified
review your final text, especially review replies and anything about
treatment, before you publish it.

All names, practices and people used as examples in this package are fictional.
