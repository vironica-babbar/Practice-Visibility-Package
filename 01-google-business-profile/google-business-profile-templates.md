# Google Business Profile Templates

Your Google Business Profile is the box that appears on Google Search and
Google Maps when someone searches your practice name or something like
"pediatrician near me." For most small practices it's the most-viewed thing
online, often more than the website.

**Where you edit it:** sign in at business.google.com, or search your practice
name on Google while signed in and click **Edit profile**.

**Before you start:** fill in `00-start-here/practice-fact-sheet.md`.

| What | Limit / rule | Last checked | Source |

No links or HTML, and no promotions or prices. | 2026-09-23 | [Google: Edit your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617) |

## Step 1: Check you're eligible (2 minutes)

Tick each box before you create or claim a profile:

- [ ] Patients visit us at this address, **or** our clinicians travel to patients.
- [ ] The address isn't a virtual office or mailbox. If it's a shared or
      co-working space, we have signage there and our staff are present
      during business hours.
- [ ] We have one profile per location. Two offices get two profiles.

> **Telehealth-only practices (common for therapists):** if you don't see
> anyone in person at a physical office, you probably aren't eligible for a
> Google Business Profile. Put your effort into specialty directories,
> insurance directories and your website instead (later modules in this package).
>
> **Therapists practicing from home:** only list your home address if clients
> actually come there. Talk to your compliance advisor about the privacy
> trade-off first.

## Step 2: Business name

Type `[PRACTICE NAME]` exactly as it appears on your sign and website.

**Don't** add a city, specialty or tagline. For example, avoid
"Example Family Pediatrics – Best Pediatrician Springfield."

**Solo clinician with a practice brand?** Google asks for one profile named
like this:

```
[PRACTICE NAME]: [PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS]
```

## Step 3: Categories

Choose **one primary category** that finishes the sentence "This practice
IS a…". Add secondary categories only if they're also true of the whole
practice. Fewer categories is better.

Type the words below into the category box and choose the closest option
Google offers. Google's category list changes, so use what appears in the box.

| Practice type | Try as primary | Possible secondary (only if true) |
|---|---|---|
| Mental health clinic | Mental health clinic *or* Psychotherapist | Psychologist, Counselor, Family counselor, Marriage counselor |
| OB/GYN clinic | Obstetrician-gynecologist | Gynecologist, Women's health clinic |
| Dermatology clinic | Dermatologist | Skin care clinic (only if you offer cosmetic services) |
| Pediatric clinic | Pediatrician | Pediatric clinic |
| Psychiatric clinic | Psychiatrist | Mental health clinic, Mental health service |
| Rehab / addiction treatment | Addiction treatment center | Rehabilitation center, Alcoholism treatment program, Drug addiction treatment center |

## Step 4: Contact details and hours

Copy these **exactly** from your fact sheet:

- **Address:** `[STREET ADDRESS]`, `[CITY]`, `[STATE]` `[ZIP]`
- **Phone:** `[PHONE]` (a local number that reaches this location, not a central call-center line if you can avoid it)
- **Website:** link to the page for **this location**. Your homepage is fine if you have only one location.
- **Appointment link:** `[BOOKING LINK]` (if you have one)
- **Hours:** use the hours your front desk or phones are staffed.
  If you're **truly by appointment only** with no set staffed hours, Google
  says to leave hours blank.
- **Holiday hours:** update these before every holiday. A listing that says
  "open" when you're closed is one of the fastest ways to lose a patient's trust.

## Step 5: Business description (copy, fill in, paste)

**Where to paste:** Edit profile → **Business information** → **Description**.

**The rule for this package: 250 characters or fewer.** Google allows up to
750, but it shows only about the first 250 before a "More" link. Staying
within 250 means patients see your whole description without clicking.
(Length doesn't change your ranking either way. See "Where keywords actually
matter on Google" below.)

**How to use:** pick the block for your practice type, fill in every `[BLANK]`,
and check the character counter in Google's editor. It must be **250 or
fewer**. If you're over, shorten in this order:
1. List fewer services (2–3 is plenty).
2. Name only your 2 largest insurance plans.
3. Drop the neighborhood or landmark.

Your full service list goes in **Services** (Step 6), your languages in
**Attributes** (Step 8), and your full insurance list on your website.

Each example below is fictional and fits within 250 characters.

### Mental health clinic

```
[PRACTICE NAME] offers therapy for [WHO YOU SEE] in [CITY], [STATE], for [2–3 CONCERNS]. In person and by telehealth. In network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.
```
*Example (226 characters): Example Counseling Group offers therapy for adults,
teens and couples in Springfield, IL, for anxiety, depression and grief. In
person and by telehealth. In network with Aetna and Cigna. If you are in
crisis, call or text 988.*

### OB/GYN clinic

```
[PRACTICE NAME] provides obstetric and gynecologic care in [CITY], [STATE], including [2–3 SERVICES]. We deliver at [HOSPITAL]. In network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]. Accepting new patients.
```
*Example (232 characters): Example Women's Health provides obstetric and
gynecologic care in Springfield, IL, including prenatal care, annual exams and
menopause care. We deliver at Springfield General. In network with Aetna and
Cigna. Accepting new patients.*

### Dermatology clinic

```
[PRACTICE NAME] is a dermatology practice in [CITY], [STATE], offering [2–3 SERVICES] for [WHO YOU SEE]. Medical visits are in network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]; cosmetic services are self-pay.
```
*Example (227 characters): Example Dermatology is a dermatology practice in
Springfield, IL, offering skin checks, acne treatment and eczema care for
adults and children. Medical visits are in network with Aetna and Cigna;
cosmetic services are self-pay.*

### Pediatric clinic

```
[PRACTICE NAME] cares for [WHO YOU SEE] in [CITY], [STATE]. We offer [2–3 SERVICES]. In network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]. Welcoming new patients, including newborns.
```
*Example (246 characters): Example Family Pediatrics cares for newborns,
children and teens in Springfield, IL. We offer well-child checkups, same-day
sick visits and sports physicals. In network with Aetna and Illinois
Medicaid. Welcoming new patients, including newborns.*

### Psychiatric clinic

```
[PRACTICE NAME] is an outpatient psychiatric practice in [CITY], [STATE], offering [2 SERVICES] for [WHO YOU SEE]. In person and by telehealth. In network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]. In crisis? Call or text 988.
```
*Example (236 characters): Example Psychiatry is an outpatient psychiatric
practice in Springfield, IL, offering evaluations and medication management
for adults and teens. In person and by telehealth. In network with Aetna and
Cigna. In crisis? Call or text 988.*

### Rehab / addiction treatment facility

```
[PRACTICE NAME] is a licensed addiction treatment [facility/program] in [CITY], [STATE], offering [LEVELS OF CARE, e.g. detox, residential and outpatient care]. In network with [2 LARGEST PLANS]. Call to talk through admission options. In crisis? Call or text 988.
```
*Example (248 characters): Example Recovery Center is a licensed addiction
treatment program in Springfield, IL, offering detox, residential and
outpatient care. In network with Aetna and Illinois Medicaid. Call to talk
through admission options. In crisis? Call or text 988.*

> **Rehab facilities:** state and federal rules on addiction treatment
> marketing are strict and differ by state. Have your compliance lead approve
> this text before publishing.

> **Long practice name?** If your name alone is over about 40 characters,
> you may need to drop a service or the insurance sentence. Keep the name
> exactly as it is; never shorten your official name to fit.

### Before you paste: 30-second check

- [ ] No `[` left anywhere in the text
- [ ] **250 characters or fewer** (check Google's counter)
- [ ] No phone numbers, links or emails (Google has separate fields for these). The 988 crisis line is included on purpose. If Google ever rejects your description, remove that sentence and keep the crisis line on your website.
- [ ] No outcome words: *cure, guaranteed, proven, best, #1, success rate*
- [ ] Every service listed is one you actually provide today
- [ ] Every insurance plan listed is one you're currently in network with
- [ ] "Accepting new patients" is only there if it's true today

## Step 6: Services

**Where to paste:** Edit profile → **Services** (sometimes shown under
**Products & services**). Add each of your `[TOP SERVICES]` as its own
service. Use this template for each one's description:

```
[SERVICE NAME] for [WHO IT IS FOR] at our [CITY] office[ and by telehealth]. [ONE SENTENCE ON WHAT THE VISIT INVOLVES]. [INSURANCE NOTE, e.g. "Covered by most plans we accept; call to confirm."]
```

Fictional example for a pediatric clinic:

```
Sports physicals for children and teens at our Springfield office. Includes a review of health history and a physical exam, with school or league forms completed at the visit. Covered by most plans we accept; call to confirm.
```

## Step 7: Photos (no patients, ever)

Upload 5–10 real photos:

- [ ] Building exterior and entrance, so patients recognize it on arrival
- [ ] Signage and suite door
- [ ] Parking, and the accessible entrance if you have one
- [ ] Waiting room (empty)
- [ ] An exam or therapy room (empty)
- [ ] Team or provider headshots (staff only, with their permission)
- [ ] Logo

**Never include:** patients (even blurred or from behind), children in the
waiting room, computer screens, schedules, whiteboards, paperwork or
before-and-after photos.

## Step 8: Attributes

Under **More** or **Attributes**, turn on everything that's true, such as
accessibility features, online appointments, telehealth / online care and
languages spoken. The options shown vary by category and change over time.

## Individual provider profiles

Google allows public-facing clinicians to have **their own profile**, separate
from the practice's.

**Should each provider have one?**
- ✅ Yes, if the provider sees patients at this location on a regular schedule.
- ❌ No profiles for support staff (front desk, billing, MAs).
- ❌ One profile per provider, not one per specialty.
- A provider may already have a profile Google created automatically. Search
  their name and location first, then claim that profile rather than making
  a duplicate.

**Provider profile name.** Name and title/degree only, with no practice name
or specialty:

```
[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS]
```

Fictional example: `Jordan Rivera, MD`

**Category:** the provider's own specialty, for example Pediatrician,
Psychiatrist, Psychologist, Dermatologist or Obstetrician-gynecologist.

**Address, phone and hours:** the practice location's details. Hours should be
the days and times this provider actually sees patients there.

### Provider description (copy, fill in, paste)

**Where to paste:** the provider's profile → Edit profile → **Description**.
**250 characters or fewer**, the same rule as the practice description.

```
[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS], is a [SPECIALTY IN PLAIN WORDS] at [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY], [STATE], caring for [WHO YOU SEE], with a focus on [1–2 FOCUS AREAS]. Speaks [PROVIDER LANGUAGES]. Accepting new patients.
```

*Fictional example (230 characters): Jordan Rivera, MD, FAAP, is a
pediatrician at Example Family Pediatrics in Springfield, IL, caring for
newborns, children and teens, with a focus on asthma and adolescent health.
Speaks English and Spanish. Accepting new patients.*

**Therapists and counselors:** use this version instead.

```
[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS], is a therapist at [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY], [STATE], working with [WHO YOU SEE] on [1–2 CONCERNS], using [1–2 APPROACHES, e.g. CBT and EMDR]. Sessions in person and by telehealth. Accepting new clients.
```

*Fictional example (219 characters): Sam Lee, LCSW, is a therapist at
Example Counseling Group in Springfield, IL, working with adults and couples
on anxiety and life changes, using CBT and EMDR. Sessions in person and by
telehealth. Accepting new clients.*

Board certification, training and a longer bio belong on the provider's page
on your website (`05-website`), not in this short description. Delete
"Accepting new patients/clients" if it isn't true today.

### When a provider leaves

Don't delete the provider's profile. Deleting it can leave an unmanaged
duplicate that Google recreates. Instead, ask Google to **mark the provider as
moved** to their new location (if they're still practicing) or **closed** (if
they've retired). Search "Google Business Profile practitioner left practice"
in Google's help center for the current steps.

## Copywriting specifics for Google Business Profile

The basics are in `00-start-here/copywriting-playbook.md`. These rules are
specific to Google.

### Where keywords actually matter on Google

| Field | Does wording affect who finds you? | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Business name** | Yes, and it's the most tempting to abuse | Real name only. Adding keywords breaks Google's rules and risks suspension. |
| **Primary category** | **Yes, strongly** | The single most important choice (Step 3). Get it right before writing anything. |
| **Services list** | **Yes.** Industry tests show adding services helps even without descriptions. | Add each service as its own item, **named in patient words**: "Skin cancer screening (skin check)", "Sports physicals", "Couples counseling". |
| **Description** | Little or no direct effect in industry tests | Write it for patients, not search engines. It's what convinces them to call. |
| **Service descriptions** | No direct effect in industry tests | Keep them short and practical (under 300 characters). |
| **Review replies** | No | Never add keywords to replies. |

### Writing a 250-character description

With only 250 characters, every word has to earn its place. Cover **who +
what + where**, then insurance, and stop:

> ✅ *"Example Family Pediatrics cares for newborns, children and teens in
> Springfield, IL. We offer well-child checkups, same-day sick visits and
> sports physicals…"* (fictional)
>
> ❌ *"Welcome! At our practice, we believe every family deserves
> compassionate, exceptional care in a warm and welcoming environment…"* (It
> uses up the 250 characters and says nothing a patient can act on.)

**Words to cut first:** "welcome," "we believe," "dedicated to,"
"compassionate," "state-of-the-art," "conveniently located." They take up
space and tell patients nothing.

### Hyperlocal on Google

- **One** neighborhood or landmark in the description is enough. The pin on
  the map already shows the location.
- Put parking, transit and accessibility details in **attributes** where
  Google offers them, and in the description only if space allows.
- If you have two offices, each profile's description names **its own**
  neighborhood. Don't copy the same text to both.

### Tone and emojis on Google
- Voice: "we" (practice) or third person (provider profiles).
- No emojis, symbols, ALL CAPS or exclamation marks, in any field.

### Services list: patient-word naming by practice type

Use these as service **names** (only if you offer them):

| Practice type | Service names |
|---|---|
| Mental health | Individual therapy · Couples counseling · Teen therapy · Grief counseling · Telehealth therapy |
| OB/GYN | Prenatal care · Well-woman exam · Birth control and IUDs · Menopause care · Gynecologic surgery |
| Dermatology | Skin cancer screening (skin check) · Acne treatment · Eczema and psoriasis care · Mole removal · Cosmetic injections |
| Pediatrics | Well-child checkups · Sick visits · Vaccinations · Sports and school physicals · Newborn care |
| Psychiatric | Psychiatric evaluation · Medication management · Child and adolescent psychiatry · Telepsychiatry |
| Rehab / addiction | Medically supervised detox · Residential treatment · Intensive outpatient program (IOP) · Medication for opioid use disorder · Family program |

### How often to touch your Google profile

| Frequency | What |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Reviews (see `02-google-reviews`) |
| Monthly | The 10-minute check below |
| Every 3 months | 1–2 new photos |
| Every year | Reread the description and services against your keyword list |
| Immediately | Holiday hours, closures, provider changes, phone changes |

> **Google Posts** (the "updates" feature) count as content marketing and
> aren't covered in this package. Industry tests found weekly posting had no
> measurable effect on local map rankings.

## Monthly 10-minute check

- [ ] Hours and holiday hours are correct
- [ ] Phone number and booking link work (test them)
- [ ] Provider profiles match who currently works here
- [ ] Insurance mentions in the description are still accurate
- [ ] Check for edits Google or the public suggested (they show as
      "updates"). Accept or reject them.
- [ ] New reviews answered with the templates in `02-google-reviews`
