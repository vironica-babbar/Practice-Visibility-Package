# Website Page Templates

Fill-in templates for the pages that make your practice findable. Each
template gives you:

- **SEO settings:** title tag, meta description and URL, which go in your
  website builder's "SEO" or "Page settings" panel
- **Page text:** paste it into the page editor, with headings marked `##`
  (make these real headings in your builder: H2, H3)

Read `website-guide.md` first. Rules that apply to every page: no emojis, no
outcome claims, no patient stories or photos, and 6th–8th grade reading level.

---

## Template 1: Provider page (one per clinician)

**SEO settings**
```
URL:              /providers/[first-last-credentials, e.g. jordan-rivera-md]
Title tag:        [PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS] | [SPECIALTY] in [CITY], [STATE]
Meta description: [PROVIDER SHORT NAME] is a [SPECIALTY IN PLAIN WORDS] at [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY], caring for [WHO YOU SEE]. [Accepting new patients.] Book online or call.
```

**Page text**
```
# [PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS]

[SPECIALTY IN PLAIN WORDS] · [PRACTICE NAME] · [CITY], [STATE]

[PASTE THE MEDIUM BIO FOR THIS PROVIDER FROM 03-specialty-directories/directory-bio-templates.md]

## Areas of focus
- [FOCUS 1]
- [FOCUS 2]
- [FOCUS 3]

## Education and training
- [DEGREE], [SCHOOL]
- Residency / training: [PROGRAM]
- [BOARD CERTIFICATION OR LICENSE, e.g. Board certified in pediatrics, American Board of Pediatrics]

## Languages
[PROVIDER LANGUAGES]

## Where and when
[PROVIDER SHORT NAME] sees patients at our [LOCATION NAME] office ([LINK TO LOCATION PAGE]) on [DAYS][, and by telehealth for patients located in STATE].

## Insurance
[PROVIDER SHORT NAME] is in network with the plans listed on our insurance page ([LINK TO INSURANCE PAGE]).

## A little more about [PROVIDER SHORT NAME] (optional)
[ONE OR TWO SENTENCES THE PROVIDER APPROVES, e.g. why they chose their field, or a hobby. Nothing about patients.]

[BUTTON: Book with PROVIDER SHORT NAME] · [BUTTON: Call PHONE]
```

**Tips**
- Use a real, recent headshot with alt text: `[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS], [SPECIALTY]`.
- Only list credentials you can verify.
- Therapists: replace "Education and training" details with your degree,
  license (type and state) and training in specific approaches (for example,
  "EMDR training, [YEAR]"). **Only list approaches you're trained in.**

## Template 2: Service page (one per main service)

**SEO settings**
```
URL:              /services/[service-in-patient-words, e.g. skin-cancer-screening]
Title tag:        [SERVICE IN PATIENT WORDS] in [CITY], [STATE] | [PRACTICE NAME]
Meta description: [SERVICE] for [WHO IT'S FOR] at [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY]. [ONE PRACTICAL DETAIL, e.g. Same-week appointments.] In network with most major plans.
```

**Page text**
```
# [SERVICE IN PATIENT WORDS]

[PRACTICE NAME] offers [SERVICE, with clinical term in brackets once if different] for [WHO IT'S FOR] at our [CITY] office[ and by telehealth].

## Who it's for
[2–3 SENTENCES IN PLAIN WORDS: who usually comes in for this, and common reasons. No promises about results.]

## What to expect
1. [STEP 1, e.g. Check in and complete a short health history.]
2. [STEP 2, e.g. Your clinician examines your skin from head to toe.]
3. [STEP 3, e.g. You talk through any spots of concern and next steps.]
[HOW LONG IT USUALLY TAKES, e.g. Most visits take about 20 minutes.]

## Who provides this service
[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS] ([LINK]) · [PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS] ([LINK])

## Insurance and cost
[e.g. This service is usually covered by insurance. See the plans we accept ([LINK]). If you are uninsured or paying yourself, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of costs.]

## Questions patients ask
**[QUESTION IN PATIENT WORDS, e.g. How often should I get a skin check?]**
[SHORT, GENERAL ANSWER, e.g. It depends on your personal and family history. Your clinician will recommend a schedule for you.]

**[QUESTION 2]**
[ANSWER]

**[QUESTION 3]**
[ANSWER]

## Book an appointment
Call [PHONE] or book online ([BOOKING LINK]). Our office is [NEIGHBORHOOD OR LANDMARK] ([LINK TO LOCATION PAGE]).

Medically reviewed by [PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS], on [DATE].
```

**Tips for the questions section**
- Use real questions callers ask your front desk. They're also what people
  type into Google and ask AI assistants.
- Keep answers general. Anything that depends on the individual should end
  with "your clinician will talk with you about…".
- Google no longer shows FAQ "rich results" (dropped May 2026), so don't add
  special FAQ code. The questions still help patients and AI assistants.

## Template 4: Insurance and payment page

**SEO settings**
```
URL:              /insurance
Title tag:        Insurance We Accept | [PRACTICE NAME], [CITY], [STATE]
Meta description: [PRACTICE NAME] is in network with [2–3 LARGEST PLANS] and more. See the full list of plans, self-pay options and how to check your coverage.
```

**Page text**
```
# Insurance and Payment

Last updated: [DATE]

## Plans we are in network with
- [INSURER NAME] – [PLAN TYPES IN NETWORK, e.g. PPO and EPO] [(not PLAN TYPES NOT IN NETWORK)]
- [INSURER NAME] – [PLAN TYPES]
- [STATE MEDICAID PROGRAM NAME, as printed on member cards] – [MANAGED CARE PLANS YOU PARTICIPATE IN]

Plans change often. Please call [PHONE] with your insurance card handy to confirm before your first visit.

## If you do not see your plan
[e.g. We may still be able to see you as an out-of-network provider. We can provide a superbill you can submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement.]

## Self-pay
[SELF-PAY NOTE]

## Your right to a Good Faith Estimate
[PASTE OR LINK THE GOOD FAITH ESTIMATE NOTICE APPROVED BY YOUR COMPLIANCE LEAD]
```

**Copywriting tips for this page**
- Write **"in network with,"** not **"we accept."** "Accept" can be read as
  in-network when you only mean you'll file claims out of network. This
  is a common source of billing complaints.
- Use insurers' **exact** names and the plan types you're in network for.
  "Blue Cross" alone is too vague, because many areas have several Blue Cross
  companies and plan types.
- Keep this page matching your insurance directory listings
  (`04-insurance-directories`). A patient who sees two different answers
  will trust neither.
- **Dermatology:** add a separate section: "Cosmetic services are not covered
  by insurance."

## Template 5: New patients page

**SEO settings**
```
URL:              /new-patients
Title tag:        New Patients | [PRACTICE NAME], [CITY], [STATE]
Meta description: [PRACTICE NAME] is [accepting new patients]. Learn how to book, what to bring and what to expect at your first visit.
```

**Page text**
```
# New Patients

[We are currently accepting new patients. / We are currently accepting new patients for PROVIDER NAMES. / We are not accepting new patients right now; call PHONE to join our waitlist.]

## How to get started
1. [Call PHONE or book online at BOOKING LINK.]
2. [Complete your intake forms through our secure patient portal before your visit.]
3. [Arrive 15 minutes early for your first appointment.]

## What to bring
- [Photo ID]
- [Insurance card]
- [List of current medications]
- [Pediatrics: your child's vaccination record]

## What to expect at your first visit
[2–3 PLAIN SENTENCES. Mental health: what a first session covers and that it is confidential. Rehab: how admissions assessments work.]

## Questions?
Call [PHONE], [HOURS].
```

> Update the first line **the same day** your status changes, and update your
> directories and insurance listings at the same time.

## Before you publish any page

- [ ] No `[` left anywhere, including the SEO fields
- [ ] Title tag about 50–60 characters; meta description 120–155
- [ ] One H1; patient phrase in the title, H1 and first paragraph
- [ ] Name, address and phone match the fact sheet exactly
- [ ] No outcome words (*cure, guaranteed, proven, best, success rate*)
- [ ] No emojis anywhere
- [ ] Every image has alt text, and no image shows a patient
- [ ] Service pages have a "Medically reviewed by" line with a date
