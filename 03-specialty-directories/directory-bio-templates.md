# Directory Bio Templates

Ready-to-fill bios for Healthgrades, WebMD Care, professional society
directories, Psychology Today and treatment locators. Fill each one once and
reuse it everywhere, so your story stays consistent.

**Before you start:** fill in `00-start-here/practice-fact-sheet.md`.

## Which length to use

Every directory has a different size box. Pick the version that fits its
character counter.

| Version | Target length | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Short** | under 300 characters | Small "about" fields and society directories |
| **Medium** | under 700 characters | Healthgrades, WebMD Care and most "bio" fields |
| **Psychology Today** | three boxes (see Part 3) | Psychology Today only |

| Limits | Last checked | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Healthgrades, WebMD Care and society directories | 2026-09-23 | These sites don't publish bio limits. Use the counter in each editor. |
| Psychology Today: Box 1 about 640, Box 2 about 360, Box 3 about 360 characters | 2026-09-23 | Third-party guides such as [Reframe Practice](https://reframepractice.com/answers/psychology-today/therapist-bio-examples). Psychology Today doesn't publish them, so confirm in your editor. |

## Rules for every bio

- ✅ Describe **who you see and what you offer.**
- ✅ Use words patients use: "anxiety," not "anxiety disorders per DSM-5-TR."
- ✅ Only list credentials you can verify (board certification, license).
- ❌ No outcome claims: *cure, guaranteed, proven, best, you will feel better.*
- ❌ No patient stories, even made-up ones presented as real.
- ❌ Don't list services, insurance or conditions you don't currently offer or treat.

## Part 1: Short bios (under 300 characters)

**Any individual provider**
```
[PROVIDER NAME], [CREDENTIALS], is a [SPECIALTY IN PLAIN WORDS] at [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY], [STATE], caring for [WHO YOU SEE] with a focus on [PROVIDER FOCUS]. Accepting new patients.
```

**Any practice or facility**
```
[PRACTICE NAME] is a [PRACTICE TYPE] in [CITY], [STATE], offering [TOP SERVICES] for [WHO YOU SEE]. In network with [INSURANCE LIST].
```

> Delete "Accepting new patients" if the provider isn't.

## Part 3: Psychology Today three-box statement

Psychology Today splits your statement into three boxes. **The first lines of
Box 1 show in search results**, so make them count. The writing speaks to the
reader as "you." That's normal on this site, but it still can't promise
outcomes.

**Where to paste:** Psychology Today → Edit profile → **Personal Statement**.

### Therapist version

**Box 1: Who you help (about 640 characters)**
```
Are you feeling [COMMON EXPERIENCE, e.g. overwhelmed, stuck or on edge] and not sure where to start? I work with [WHO YOU SEE] facing [PROVIDER FOCUS, e.g. anxiety, depression, life transitions and grief]. Many people I see are [RELATABLE SITUATION, e.g. juggling work and family, adjusting to a big change, or noticing old patterns repeating]. Therapy offers a confidential space to talk openly, understand what is going on, and work on the changes that matter to you, at a pace that feels manageable.
```

**Box 2: How you work (about 360 characters)**
```
My approach is [STYLE, e.g. warm, collaborative and practical]. I draw on [APPROACHES, e.g. CBT, ACT and EMDR] and tailor sessions to your goals. We'll start by getting to know what brings you in and what you'd like to be different, then build a plan together.
```

**Box 3: About you (about 360 characters)**
```
I am a [LICENSE TYPE] licensed in [STATE] with [NUMBER] years of experience in [SETTINGS, e.g. community mental health and private practice]. I offer sessions in person at our [CITY] office and by telehealth, in [PROVIDER LANGUAGES]. I am in network with [INSURANCE LIST].
```

> **Box 3 too long?** A long insurance list is usually why. Psychology Today
> has separate insurance checkboxes, so you can replace the last sentence
> with: `I am in network with several insurance plans (listed below).`

### Psychiatrist / psychiatric NP version

**Box 1**
```
If you are looking for a psychiatric evaluation or support with medication, I work with [WHO YOU SEE] experiencing [PROVIDER FOCUS, e.g. depression, anxiety, ADHD and bipolar disorder]. Some people come to me for a first evaluation; others want a second opinion or a new prescriber. I take time to understand your history and goals and explain options clearly, so you can make informed decisions about your care.
```

**Box 2**
```
Appointments begin with a thorough evaluation. Together we discuss treatment options, which may include medication and referrals for therapy, and review how things are going at regular follow-ups. I coordinate with your therapist or primary care clinician when you'd like me to.
```

**Box 3**
```
I am a [CREDENTIALS, e.g. board-certified psychiatrist] licensed in [STATE] with [NUMBER] years of experience. I see patients in person in [CITY] and by telehealth, in [PROVIDER LANGUAGES]. I am in network with [INSURANCE LIST].
```

### Treatment center version

**Box 1**
```
[PRACTICE NAME] provides [LEVELS OF CARE, e.g. medically supervised detox, residential and intensive outpatient] treatment for [WHO YOU SEE] experiencing [CONCERNS, e.g. alcohol and opioid use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions]. Reaching out can feel like a big step. Our admissions team can answer questions about programs, insurance and what to expect, confidentially and without obligation.
```

**Box 2**
```
Care is provided by a team of [TEAM, e.g. physicians, nurses and licensed counselors]. Programs include [SERVICES, e.g. individual and group therapy, medication for opioid use disorder, family programming and discharge planning], based on an assessment of each person's needs.
```

**Box 3**
```
[PRACTICE NAME] is licensed by [STATE FACILITY LICENSE][ and accredited by ACCREDITATION]. We are located in [CITY], [STATE] and are in network with [INSURANCE LIST]. [CRISIS LINE TEXT]
```

## Copywriting specifics for directories

The basics are in `00-start-here/copywriting-playbook.md`. These rules are
specific to directories.

### Keywords: the checkboxes matter more than the bio

On Psychology Today, Healthgrades, WebMD and the society directories,
patients mostly search using **filters**: specialty, conditions treated,
insurance, language, telehealth, age group. Those filters come from the
**boxes you tick**, not from your bio.

1. **Fill in every structured field first:** conditions, services,
   insurance, languages, ages, telehealth, accepting new patients.
2. **Where a directory lets you choose "top" or featured specialties**
   (Psychology Today does), pick the 2–3 you most want to be found for, and
   make sure your bio's first sentence mentions the same ones.
3. **Tick only what's true.** Ticking every condition gets you more searches
   and more of the wrong callers, and on insurer-linked sites it can become a
   compliance problem.
4. **Use the patient phrase in the bio's first sentence** ("anxiety,"
   "skin checks," "kids"), because many directories show that sentence in
   search results.

### Voice by directory

| Directory | Voice | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| Psychology Today (individual) | First person, speaking to "you" | "I work with adults who feel stuck…" |
| Psychology Today (group / treatment center) | "We" | "Our team provides…" |
| Healthgrades, WebMD Care | Third person | "Dr. Rivera cares for…" |
| ACOG, AAD, AAP, APA, AACAP | Third person, most formal | "Jordan Rivera, MD, FAAP, is a board-certified pediatrician…" |

### Hyperlocal in directories

- The address field handles location. Use the bio for details a map can't
  show: "free parking behind the building," "near the Main Street bus stop."
- **Telehealth:** say exactly where you can see clients ("clients anywhere in
  Illinois"). Directories often show telehealth providers to searchers
  statewide, so this line prevents out-of-state calls.
- **Multiple offices:** list each address in its own location field, and name
  the days you're at each one.

### Consistency rules (copy these exactly everywhere)

- **Credentials** written one way: "LCSW," not "L.C.S.W." in one place and
  "LCSW" in another. List them in the same order everywhere.
- **Headshot:** the same recent, professional photo on every directory and
  on Google. Update it every few years.
- **Name:** the same form everywhere (for example, always "Jordan Rivera, MD";
  never "Dr. Jordan A. Rivera" on one site and "J. Rivera" on another).

### Emojis and formatting
No emojis, symbols, ALL CAPS or bullet characters inside bio boxes. Many
directories strip formatting, so symbols often turn into odd characters.
Use short paragraphs instead.

### How often to update directories

| Frequency | What |
|---|---|
| **Immediately** | "Accepting new patients/clients" status. This is the most-used filter, and a wrong answer wastes a patient's call. |
| Every 3 months | Insurance checkboxes (after any contract change) |
| Every 6 months | Full review with the checklist in `directory-guide.md` |
| Every year | Refresh the bio: new training, new services, updated keywords |

## Before you paste: 30-second check

- [ ] No `[` left anywhere
- [ ] Fits the directory's character counter
- [ ] No outcome words: *cure, guaranteed, proven, best, success rate, you will feel better*
- [ ] Every credential, service and insurance plan is current and accurate
- [ ] Practice name, address and phone copied exactly from the fact sheet
- [ ] For rehab facilities: approved by your compliance lead (addiction treatment marketing is tightly regulated in many states)
