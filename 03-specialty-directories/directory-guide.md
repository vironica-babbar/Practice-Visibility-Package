# Specialty Directories: Which Ones, In What Order

Patients don't only search Google. Many start on a health directory like
Psychology Today, Healthgrades or their doctor's professional society
website. This guide tells you **which directories matter for your practice
type** and how to get listed on each one.

The text you paste into these directories (bios and descriptions) is in
`directory-bio-templates.md` in this folder.

---

## Step 0: Fix your source data first (all practice types)

Many directories **copy your information automatically** from the national
NPI Registry run by CMS. If your NPI record is wrong, the mistake spreads to
dozens of sites, and fixing each site one by one won't stick.

**Do this before anything else:**

1. Search each provider and the practice at **npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov**.
2. Check the practice address, phone and specialty (called "taxonomy") on each record.
3. If anything is wrong, the provider (or an authorized surrogate) logs in at
   **nppes.cms.hhs.gov** and updates it. CMS asks for changes to be reported
   within 30 days.
4. Write the date you checked in the tracker at the bottom of this page.

allows. **Membership** means you must belong to that professional society to

## Step 2: How to get listed

### Healthgrades and WebMD Care (free, all practice types)
1. Search for the provider's name on the site. A profile probably already
   exists, built automatically from NPI data.
2. Click **"Is this you?"**, **"Claim your profile"** or similar, and follow
   the verification steps.
3. Fill in every field using your fact sheet: insurance, languages,
   locations, conditions treated.
4. Paste a bio from `directory-bio-templates.md`.
5. Both sites show patient reviews. Use `02-google-reviews/review-reply-library.md`
   for any replies. The same HIPAA rules apply.

### Psychology Today (mental health, psychiatric, rehab)
1. Sign up at psychologytoday.com with the profile type that fits:
   therapist, psychiatrist or treatment center.
2. Have `[LICENSE TYPE, NUMBER AND STATE]` ready. Psychology Today verifies licenses.
3. Paste the three-box statement from `directory-bio-templates.md`.
4. Tick the issues, insurance and approaches **you actually treat and accept.**
   Ticking extra boxes to show up in more searches wastes clients' time and
   can mislead them.

### Professional society directories (ACOG, AAD, AAP, APA, AACAP)
1. Log in to the society's member account.
2. Find the public profile or referral directory setting and **opt in** if
   there is one (the American Psychiatric Association requires this).
3. Copy the address, phone and hours **exactly** from your fact sheet.
4. Paste the short or medium bio from `directory-bio-templates.md` if the
   directory has a bio field.

### FindTreatment.gov (rehab facilities; licensed mental health clinics)
1. **Check eligibility.** Your facility must be licensed, certified or
   otherwise approved by the state substance use or mental health authority.
   Individual practitioners and small private group practices not licensed as
   a clinic aren't eligible.
2. **Search for yourself** at findtreatment.gov. You may already be listed.
3. **To be added or update your details:** use the facility registration form
   on FindTreatment.gov, email **FindTreatment@samhsa.hhs.gov**, or call the
   BHSIS Project Office at **1-833-888-1553** (weekdays).
4. **Stay listed:** once listed, you'll get SAMHSA's annual N-SUMHSS survey
   each spring. **You must complete it to stay listed.** Put it on your calendar.

### Zocdoc (optional, paid)
Zocdoc charges per new-patient booking, and the fee varies by specialty and
location. It helps visibility only if you have **open appointment slots** to
show. Ask for a written quote and compare it with what a new patient is worth
to your practice before signing up.
