# Apple Maps and Bing Places Guide

Google isn't the only map. **Apple Maps** is the built-in map on every
iPhone, and Siri uses it when someone asks "find a pediatrician near me."
**Bing** powers Microsoft's search and Copilot assistant, and industry
researchers report it's also one of the sources ChatGPT uses for local
answers. Both are free, and setting them up takes about an hour total.

**Do Google first** (`01-google-business-profile`). Bing can copy it.

## Part 1: Bing Places (do this first, about 20 minutes)

### Step 1: Import from Google
1. Go to **bingplaces.com** and sign in with a Microsoft, Google or Facebook
   account. **Use a shared practice account** (for example, one owned by the
   practice manager role), not a staff member's personal account.
2. New account: choose **Import from Google Business Profile**. Existing
   account: choose **Add** and then the import option.
3. Sign in to the Google account that manages your Google profile, and select
   the locations to import.
4. Wait for Bing's review, usually around 10 days.

**If the import fails** (it sometimes does), choose **Add a new business**
and enter the details by hand from your fact sheet. Bing then asks you to
verify by phone, email or postcard.

### Step 2: Check what was imported
The import copies whatever is on Google, **including mistakes.** Check every
field against the fact sheet:

- [ ] Business name: exactly `[PRACTICE NAME]`, no keywords
- [ ] Address, including suite, written exactly as on the fact sheet
- [ ] Phone: `[PHONE]`
- [ ] Website: the location page (or homepage for one office)
- [ ] Categories: the closest match to your Google category. Bing's list
      uses different names, so search for words like "pediatrician,"
      "psychiatrist," "dermatologist," "counselor" or "addiction treatment."
- [ ] Hours, including holiday hours
- [ ] Photos: no patients (same rules as Google)
- [ ] Description (see copywriting below)

### Step 3: Keep it in sync
Bing doesn't reliably update itself when you change Google. **Whenever you
change Google, change Bing the same day.** Put both on the same checklist.

## Part 2: Apple Business, for Apple Maps (about 30 minutes)

### Step 1: Sign in and find your place
1. Go to **business.apple.com** and sign in with an Apple Account. **Use a
   practice-owned Apple Account**, not a staff member's personal one.
2. Search for your practice. Apple Maps may already have a listing built from
   other data sources. **Claim it** rather than creating a new one, so you
   don't end up with a duplicate.
3. If there's no listing, add your practice using the fact sheet.

### Step 2: Verify
Apple verifies ownership in one of several ways: an automated phone call
to the listed number (usually fastest), email, or business documents. **Make
sure someone will answer the practice phone** during verification, because
an automated attendant may block the call. If that happens, choose another
verification method.

### Step 3: Complete the place card
The place card is Apple's version of your Google profile.

- [ ] Name, address, phone and website exactly as on the fact sheet
- [ ] Primary category plus any accurate secondary categories
- [ ] Hours and holiday hours
- [ ] Logo and 5–10 photos (entrance, signage, empty waiting room, team
      headshots with permission, **no patients**)
- [ ] Attributes Apple offers, such as accessibility, parking and payment
      types (only if true)
- [ ] **Action links:** if Apple offers a "Book" or "Schedule" button, link
      it to `[BOOKING LINK]` (only if patients can actually book online)
- [ ] Description or "About" text, if the field is available for your
      category (see copywriting below)

### What to skip
Apple Business also offers **Showcases** (promotional posts), branded email
and Maps ads. These are marketing features outside this package's scope. They
aren't needed for your practice to be found.

### Individual provider listings
Individual clinician listings are common on Google but much less so on Apple
and Bing. **Focus on the practice listing on both platforms.** If you find an
existing Apple or Bing listing for an individual provider, make sure it's
accurate (right address and phone), or report it if the provider has left.

## Practice-type notes

| Practice type | Watch out for |
|---|---|
| Mental health (therapists) | Solo therapists working from home or telehealth-only: the same eligibility caution as Google applies. Only list an address where clients are actually seen. |
| OB/GYN | Make sure Apple and Bing show **your office**, not the hospital's labor and delivery unit, and not the reverse |
| Dermatology | If your cosmetic services have a separate brand and address, keep them as separate, consistent listings. Don't merge them. |
| Pediatrics | After-hours or urgent care numbers belong in your website text, not as the main phone on map listings |
| Psychiatric | Check that the category says psychiatrist or mental health, not a general medical clinic |
| Rehab / addiction | Search Apple and Bing Maps for your name. Report any listing showing your name with **someone else's phone number.** This is a known problem in addiction treatment. |

## Copywriting specifics for Apple and Bing

The basics are in `00-start-here/copywriting-playbook.md`.

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Description** | Reuse your Google description. Where the field is shorter (some reports say Bing shows only the first ~200 characters of imported text), keep **who + what + where** in the first 200 characters, as you already did for Google's first 250. |
| **Keywords** | Your **category** matters most, just as on Google. Never add keywords to the business name. |
| **Hyperlocal** | Same neighborhood or landmark as Google. Don't add towns you don't serve. |
| **Voice** | "We" (practice). Same tone as your Google description. |
| **Emojis / symbols** | None, in any field |
| **Consistency** | Everything **character for character** identical to Google and your website: suite format, phone format, hours |

### How often

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Same day as any change on Google** | Make the same change on Bing and Apple |
| **Before every holiday** | Holiday hours on Google, Bing **and** Apple |
| **Monthly** | Look up your practice in Apple Maps and Bing Maps on a phone, and check that the name, hours and phone are right |
| **Every 6 months** | Full comparison against the fact sheet (see `08-listing-consistency`) |

## 10-minute check: are you on all three maps?

On a phone:
- [ ] **Google Maps:** search `[PRACTICE NAME] [CITY]`. Correct?
- [ ] **Apple Maps:** same search. Correct? Claimed?
- [ ] **Bing Maps** (bing.com/maps): same search. Correct? Claimed?
- [ ] Ask Siri: "What are the hours for `[PRACTICE NAME]` in `[CITY]`?"
- [ ] Tap **Call** and **Directions** on each one. Do they work?
