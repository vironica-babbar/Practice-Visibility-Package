# "Practice at a Glance" Page Template

One page on your website that states your key facts in plain question-and-
answer form. It helps:

- **Patients** who want quick answers
- **AI assistants** (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot),
  which quote short, clear facts
- **Your team**, as one place to check the facts are current

**Where it goes:** a page on your website, linked from the footer on every page.
**How to fill it:** copy answers from `00-start-here/practice-fact-sheet.md`.
Delete any section that doesn't apply. Keep the practice name in the answers
rather than "we," because AI may quote a single sentence on its own.

---

## SEO settings

```
URL:              /about/practice-facts
Title tag:        [PRACTICE NAME] at a Glance | [CITY], [STATE]
Meta description: Quick facts about [PRACTICE NAME] in [CITY]: services, providers, hours, insurance, location and new patient status.
```

- [ ] Every "as of" date is this month
- [ ] Facts match your Google profile, directories and insurance listings **exactly**
- [ ] The practice name is used in answers instead of "we"
- [ ] No outcome words, no emojis, no patient information
- [ ] Linked from the footer on every page
